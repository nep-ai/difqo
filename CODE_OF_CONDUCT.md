# Code of Conduct

Help make Difqo a welcoming place to build and learn. Treat people with respect, welcome different levels of experience and perspectives, and give constructive feedback about the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and publishing someone’s private information without permission are unacceptable.

This applies in Difqo’s project spaces and when someone represents the project. To report a concern privately, email the [Nep AI team](mailto:info@nepai.com.np) with “Difqo code of conduct” in the subject. Include relevant links when you can. The team will limit sharing to people who need to handle the report. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation to address violations.
